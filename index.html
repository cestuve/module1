Interactive Client Simulation

Introduction to Addiction & Case Management

In this interactive simulation, you’ll step into the role of a chemical dependency counselor assistant (CDCA) trainee and follow one client’s case from intake through case management.

As you work with Dylan, you’ll make decisions, respond to real-world situations, and explore how addiction, stigma, respectful communication, and access to resources can affect a person’s recovery. Along the way, you’ll practice the foundational knowledge and skills a CDCA uses to support clients with compassion, professionalism, and purpose.
